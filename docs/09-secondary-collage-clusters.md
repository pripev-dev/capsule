# 09 – Secondary collage clusters

Modern editorial pages now place the contract's vertical rails, horizontal
dividers and side spacers. Previously only opening-banner clusters reached
layout. The engine selects no material: it packs the declared distinct members
with the cluster's density and seed at its named reading anchor.

Members enter the ordinary contour blockers individually. No rectangular
selection envelope obstructs the words. Intentional overlap inside one heap
is permitted; collisions between unrelated placements remain refusals. Print
pagination moves each complete heap together. Legacy editions retain their
previous geometry.

Phone, tablet and print drop policies are honoured. Simplification reduces
the occupied zone while retaining every declared member. Desktop does not
inherit a tablet-only policy.

Synthetic tests cover distinct membership, deterministic contours, missing
anchors, surface policies, changed reading flow at narrow and wide widths,
and a shared print sheet. These prove the layout path, not visual acceptance.
The composition agent's current visual-plan tool still emits opening banners
and individual accents; secondary editorial selection and real family renders
are the next integration work. No private candidate has been rewritten.
