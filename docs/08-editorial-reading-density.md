# 08 – Editorial reading density

Revision 3 documents carry explicit editorial type roles. Their printed body
uses a physical 12–13pt reading range rather than inheriting the larger screen
measure. Display, section and caption roles retain their hierarchy; the 12pt
physical floor remains authoritative. Earlier prototype editions retain their
measured geometry.

Modern collapsed voice areas reserve the renderer's padding and control rows.
An empty heading no longer reserves a full caption row. Expanded transcripts
still contribute their measured text height, and player controls remain in the
screen model. Print removes players through the application's existing print
projection; source recordings and voice blocks remain in the durable capsule.

The geometry is deterministic. No change to source text, font identifiers,
palettes, visual approvals or editorial membership is required. Surface tests
cover A4 and Letter legibility and collapsed versus expanded voice areas.
Rendered comparison remains necessary: these checks do not establish a
finished cookbook aesthetic or predict every family's page count.
